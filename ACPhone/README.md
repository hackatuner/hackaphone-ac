# HackaPhone v1.1.2

An iPhone-style phone for Assetto Corsa (Custom Shaders Patch Lua app). Everything is custom-drawn: no stock AC or ImGui boxes.

## New in v1.3.0
- **Three home pages.** Swipe between them. Car is the first app on page 2.
- **Edit your home screen:** press and hold any app until it wiggles, then drag it to reorder or onto another page (drag to the screen edge to turn the page). Tap the minus to remove it; removed apps wait in a tray to add back. Tap Done when finished.
- **Race Board:** podiums for most wins, fastest 0-60 mph and fastest 0-200 km/h.
- Fonts now actually change; hold-to-flash; stronger headlight and tail light colour.

## New in v1.2.0
- **Car app**: headlights, high beams, hazards, flash, daytime lights, driver door, headlight and tail light colours (tinted glow), plus bonnet, boot and extra doors when the car defines them as switches.
- Phone stays open during races, live lap starts on movement, 15 fonts, Cut-Up standings.

## New in v1.1.2
- **Crashing banks your points:** a crash ends the run but every point is banked, counted on the leaderboards, your PB and your cash. Ghost Shield now soaks up one crash and keeps your run going.

## New in v1.1
- **Race mode:** challenge any friend with HackaPhone to a race from the Race app or their profile. Pick 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 or 1 mile, 1 km, a custom distance, or a speed pull (0-100, 0-200, 0-300, 100-200, 60-130, full pull or your own). They get a notification and a message, and can accept or decline. Add a cash wager and the winner takes the pot.
- **Countdown and live tracker:** a 5-second countdown with false-start penalty, then a live HUD showing both cars, the gap, time and speed. The phone gets out of the way and comes back with the result.
- **Full timing sheet:** total time, trap speed, top speed, average, reaction and splits (60 ft, 100 m, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 mile, 1 km, 0-100, 0-200 and more).
- **Solo runs and records:** arm a solo run any time. Your best time per format and car is saved. Win/loss record, streak, race history and rematch.

## New in v1.0
- **Power-Ups:** a power-up market with 2x, 3x, 5x, 7x and 10x score multipliers plus creative boosts: Ghost Shield (survive a crash), Traffic Magnet, Hyper Combo, Time Warp, Lucky Charm (jackpots), Overdrive, Cash Cow, Quick Bank, Head Start, PB Hunter and Mystery Crates. One multiplier and two boosts per run, used up when the run starts.
- **Bank:** you earn cash for every run (banked pays more, crashes still pay). Card with your balance, savings that earn interest even while you're offline, send money to friends, activity log and a richest-drivers list.
- **Market:** a live token exchange (Cut Coin, Nitro Bond, Grip Index, same prices for the whole server), player-to-player trading of power-ups and tokens, titles and permanent upgrades.
- **Runs:** every Cut-Up run is logged with the car you drove, track, top speed, average speed, multiplier, near misses and cash. A Garage tab shows how each car performs.
- **Vault:** play your own unreleased music in the game, with search, a play queue (Up Next), shuffle and repeat. Point it at the folder where you save tracks from Untitled. It controls the lock screen, island and Control Centre too.
- **Camera angles:** every Video angle is fully editable (distance, height, speed, lens, aim point, roll, shake) with live preview and saved angles.
- **Welcome screen:** a banner or full phone takeover greets you when you join, with your own message and stats. New players get a welcome in chat. Personalize › Welcome Screen.
- **Album art wallpaper:** pick Actual art, Blurred, or Art + blur, with a blur slider.

## New in v0.9
- **Cut-Up redesign:** the action bar and the Cut-Up app glow in the colour of your current tier, scores count up, and there is a tier ladder and a live event feed. Crashes shake and flash red; banks flash green.
- **Live boards:** when anyone crashes or banks, everyone's Cut-Up scores and podium update right away. The podium columns slide into their new places, and crash/bank tags show on each driver. Also in Leaderboards › Cut-Up.

## New in v0.8
- **4K liveries:** paint is now rendered on your graphics card into a 4096×4096 texture with MIP maps, so it's sharp up close and clean at a distance. Edges are anti-aliased.
- **Camo fixed:** real organic woodland camo (four tones, smooth natural shapes) instead of blocks. New **Urban** (grey), **Digital** (pixel) and **Tiger** camo, plus **Hex**.
- **Realistic carbon:** 2×2 twill weave with shaded fibre tows. **Forged carbon** now shows real chopped-fibre flakes.
- **Paint finishes in the texture:** Metallic and Candy get real flake sparkle, Pearl gets a colour shift, and Candy gets deeper, richer colour.
- The car preview in the Livery app now shows the actual texture.

## New in v0.7
- **Resize the phone:** drag the frame's right, left or bottom edge, or the bottom-right corner. It keeps its proportions, so nothing stretches or breaks, and text stays sharp at every size. There's also a size slider and S/M/L/XL presets in Personalize › Size.
- **Move the phone:** right-click and drag anywhere on it, long-press the status bar, or drag the frame.
- **Action bar size** slider in Personalize › Size.

## New in v0.6.2
- **Album art wallpaper:** Personalize › Wallpaper › Music makes the cover of the current song your wallpaper. It cross-fades when the track changes, and you can blur it or keep it sharp.

## New in v0.6.1
- Music controls fixed: play/pause, next and previous now use CSP's built-in media controls, so they work with Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music and other Windows players.

## New in v0.6
- **Leaderboards:** server standings with a top-3 podium and gaps to the leader (laps in races), a lap-time ranking, Cut-Up boards (Live, Session, All-time) and drift points. Everyone on the server is included.
- Fixed a Music app crash on tracks without an album name.

- **Personalize:** pick a theme (3 new sleek ones: Graphite, Aurora, Carbon), a wallpaper (theme art, your car's photo, the track, or any 4K image from your PC), a font (Modern, Classic or Sport), an app icon style (Colour, Glass or Minimal), bold text, theme symbols, accent colour, which apps show on the home screen, and the action bar style.
- **Music:** new Music app with album art, progress and play/pause/next/previous/volume. Controls also appear on the lock screen, in Control Centre, in the Dynamic Island and on the home screen.
- **Skins:** big preview of what you're wearing, search, favourites, a Random button, and a Friends tab.
- **Livery:** Racing stripes, Diagonal, Split and Forged carbon patterns. Satin and Candy finishes. Saved looks, plus share codes you can copy and paste through chat.
- **Cut-Up:** combo tiers (Heating Up, On Fire, Untouchable, God Mode), a last-run summary, and a redesigned action bar (Pro or Minimal) with a PB progress line.

## New in v0.4
- **Cut-Up:** a No Hesi-style points system. Weave through traffic for Near Miss, Close Call, Overtake, Oncoming, Lane Split, Slipstream and Speed Trap points. Your multiplier goes up to x20. Slow down to bank your run; crash and you lose it. There's a live leaderboard with everyone on HackaPhone (Live, Session and All-time).
- **Action bar:** an on-screen Cut-Up HUD with your score, a multiplier ring with the combo timer, speed, PB and pop-up events. Drag it wherever you want.
- **Themes:** 9 looks, each with animated wallpaper art: Classic, Blood Moon (skulls), Gold Rush (guns and a hazard stripe), Neon Tokyo (synthwave grid), Toxic, Inferno, Frostbite, Cash Money and Midnight JDM. Includes a **Thick UI** mode.
- **Software Update:** the phone updates itself when a new version is published (Settings › Software Update).

## New in v0.3
- **Lock screen** with a big clock, a live lap card, Now Playing and your latest notifications. Swipe up to open.
- **Live updates:** friends joining or leaving, every lap, personal bests, contact with other cars, low fuel, position changes, weather changes and new sessions.
- **Dynamic Island Live Activity** shows your lap timer and delta while you drive, or REC, Walk or music. Tap it to expand.
- **Notification Centre:** tap the left side of the status bar or pull down from the top-left.
- **Control Centre:** tap the right side of the status bar. It has Chase Cam, Do Not Disturb, notifications, Live Activities, Now Playing, Clean screen, Walk, Camera, Lock, server info, HUD and Maps.
- **Now Playing:** whatever is playing in Windows (Spotify, YouTube and so on) shows on the lock screen, in Control Centre, in the island and on home page 2.
- **Server chat:** the real AC chat now appears in Messages, and you can reply from the phone.

## Apps
| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Maps** | Live track map with you and your friends on it. Drag, zoom, follow, and tap a friend to watch or text them. Teleport list for servers that have teleport points. |
| **Livery** | Repaint your car live with a main colour, a second colour and patterns (Fade, Stripes, Checker, Camo, Carbon). Finishes: Gloss, Matte, Metallic, Pearl, Chrome. Paint rims and calipers too. Choose a full repaint or a tint over your skin. Friends see it online. |
| **HUD** | Switch your HUD apps on and off, use Clean screen, and save or load full layouts (which apps are open and where). |
| **Messages** | Group chat with everyone on the server, plus private chats. Banners pop in when messages arrive. |
| **Friends** | Who's on the server, their car, status and distance. Profile pages with Message, Honk, Watch and Star. |
| **Camera** | Free photo camera. WASD/QE to fly, right mouse to look, scroll to zoom, then press the shutter. The phone hides itself for the shot. |
| **Walk** | Step out next to your car and walk around in first person. WASD, Shift to run, Space to jump. |
| **Video** | Cinematic shots for filming friends: Orbit, Drone, Tracking, Flyby, Low Chase, Handheld. Press REC, and the phone shrinks to a REC bar. Capture with OBS or ShadowPlay. |
| **Chase Cam** | Adjustable chase camera with presets (Street, Drift, Low, Cinema) and sliders for distance, height, FOV, stiffness, look ahead, speed zoom and corner tilt. It can replace only AC's chase view. |
| **Plates** | Custom license plates with a live preview, flags, quick picks, favourites and recents. Syncs online. Works with Hacka Plates. |
| **Skins** | Browse your car's skins with preview images and swap in-game. Syncs online. Works with Skin Companion. |
| **Weather** | Live conditions: air and road temperature, wind, rain, wetness and time. Offline time slider, plus one tap to open Dream Realism Weather or Pure Planner. |
| **Apps** | Your App Library: switch any installed app on or off. Star apps to put them on the home screen (swipe left). |
| **Bank / Market / Power-Ups** | Money, savings, live exchange, player trading and the power-up shop. |
| **Race** | Challenge friends to timed races and pulls, solo runs, records. |
| **Runs** | Your Cut-Up run log and garage. |
| **Vault** | Your own music with queue and search. |
| **Settings** | Status, notifications, phone hotkey, wallpaper and accent colour. |

## Using it
- Open it from the app bar on the right of the screen, or press **Ctrl+P** (change the key in Settings › Phone key).
- To go home, tap or swipe up on the bar at the bottom of the screen. To go back, tap the **‹** at the top left.
- Drag the phone by its top edge or frame.

## Install (everyone in the crew)
Copy the `ACPhone` folder into `assettocorsa/apps/lua/`, so you have `assettocorsa/apps/lua/ACPhone/manifest.ini`.

## Notes
- Everyone needs HackaPhone installed to message each other. Plates and skins also sync with friends using Hacka Plates or Skin Companion.
- Only one camera mode runs at a time. Chase Cam steps aside while you use Camera, Walk or Video.
- Errors: open CSP's Lua Debug app, or check `Documents/Assetto Corsa/logs/custom_shaders_patch.log`.
